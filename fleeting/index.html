This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application and web experience FLEETING (Android package name com.varlextech.fleeting, the "App"), developed and published by Varlextech Limited ("Varlextech Limited", "we", "us", or "our"). It explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • FLEETING collects no personal data. It is a self-contained life-simulation game: everything it records — lives lived, choices made, memorial stars, language preference, and a local event log — is stored on your device only.
  • The App contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash-reporting, and no tracking SDKs. Varlextech Limited operates no game servers and receives no data from your gameplay.
  • Sharing is optional and user-initiated: share links encode only game data (a world seed and your choices), never your identity.
  • You can delete all data the App stores by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

2. Information stored on your device

FLEETING saves the following data locally on your device (in browser/app-local storage) so the App can function and remember your progress and preferences:

  • Game progress — your current life, past lives, memorial stars, the letter for the next life, generation chain, and worlds-lived collection.
  • Settings — language preference, audio mute state.
  • Local event log — a small log of in-app actions (for example, "app opened" or "share tapped") used to improve the game. It is capped at 500 entries, never leaves your device, and is not accessible to us.
  • Offline cache — game assets cached by a service worker so the App works without a network connection. No personal data is cached.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

3. Device permissions

The Android App requests a single permission:

  • Internet access — required so the embedded WebView can load game assets and open share links. It is not used to transmit any of your data to us or to third parties.

The App requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or device identifiers.

4. Sharing feature

When you choose to share (via your device's share sheet or by copying a link), the App generates a URL on-device that encodes game data only: a world seed, your in-game choices, your birthplace result, age at death, and an optional epitaph line you typed yourself. These links contain no name, account, device identifier, or any other personal information.

When someone opens a shared link, our hosting provider's edge network serves them the game and a preview image. Standard, ephemeral CDN request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the page; we do not build profiles from them. See Section 5.

5. Third-party services

The App bundles no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. There are no ads and no purchases, so no payment or ad-network data is ever processed.

The only third party involved in operating the App is our website hosting provider (Vercel Inc.), which serves the game files and shared-link preview pages over HTTPS and may retain short-lived server access logs under its own privacy policy:

  • Vercel Privacy Policy: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

6. Information Varlextech Limited does not collect

We do not ourselves collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Account credentials (the App has no sign-up or login)
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Photos, media, or files on your device
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Payment card details (the App has no purchases)
  • Any analytics or gameplay telemetry — the event log described in Section 2 stays on your device

7. Data sharing and sale

Varlextech Limited does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone, and does not transmit data to third parties for their own marketing. Because the App collects nothing, there is nothing to share.

8. Data retention and deletion

All data the App stores remains only on your device and is erased when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. We hold no server-side copy of any of it, so no request to us is needed — though you may contact us at varlextechltd@gmail.com with any privacy question and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent.

  • Delete your data: uninstall the App or use "Clear data"/"Clear storage" in your device's app settings — this removes everything the App stores.
  • Control sharing: share links are generated only when you tap a share button; nothing is shared automatically.
  • Contact us: for any privacy request, email varlextechltd@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

EEA/UK users may also lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

10. Children's privacy

FLEETING depicts a whole human life — including illness, hardship, and death — in abstract form, and is intended for players aged 15 and older. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone of any age.

11. Security

Because all app data is stored locally on your device and Varlextech Limited operates no game servers, the primary safeguard is your device's own security (device lock, OS sandboxing). The game files and share-link previews are served to you over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

12. International data transfers

We do not transfer your personal data internationally because we do not collect it. Our hosting provider may process ephemeral request logs on servers in various countries under the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.
