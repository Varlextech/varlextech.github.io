This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application QRForge (Android package name com.varlextech.qrforge, the "App"), developed and published by Varlextech Limited ("Varlextech Limited", "we", "us", or "our"). It explains what information the App handles, how it is used, the third-party services integrated into the App, and the choices you have.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • QRForge is an offline-first QR & barcode toolkit. Your scan history, created codes, and settings are stored locally on your device only. Varlextech Limited operates no servers and does not itself collect, transmit, or store your personal data.
  • Camera frames and photos you select are decoded on your device and are never uploaded to us.
  • The App shows advertising via Google AdMob and offers an optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase processed by Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit. These third-party services may collect device and usage data under their own privacy policies, as detailed in Section 5.
  • You can delete all data the App stores by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

2. Information stored on your device

QRForge saves the following data locally on your device (in app-private storage) so the App can function and remember your history and preferences:

  • Scan history — the decoded text content of codes you scan (which may include URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, contact details, email, phone, SMS, geo, or event data embedded in those codes), along with timestamps and favorite flags. Capped at 100 entries.
  • Created history — the payloads of QR codes you generate in the App (text, URLs, Wi-Fi, email, phone, SMS, contact, or geo templates). Capped at 100 entries.
  • Settings — theme preference (light/dark/system), haptic feedback toggle, continuous-scan mode, and enabled barcode formats.
  • Purchase entitlement — a flag recording whether the "Remove Ads" purchase is active.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Varlextech Limited. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Codes you scan or create can contain sensitive information (for example, Wi-Fi passwords or contact details). This content is stored only in the App's private, on-device history and is never transmitted to us.

3. Device permissions

The App requests the following device permissions, used solely to provide its features. All processing happens on your device:

  • Camera — required to scan QR codes and barcodes. Live preview frames are analyzed in real time on your device; they are not recorded, saved, or transmitted.
  • Photo library (read) — used only when you choose to scan a code from an existing photo. The selected image is decoded on-device and is not uploaded. On Android 14+ and iOS, the App accesses only the items you explicitly select where the OS supports limited access.
  • Photo library (add/save) — used only when you tap to save a generated QR code image to your photo library.

Denying the camera permission disables live scanning; the generator and history features continue to work. You can grant or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

4. Information Varlextech Limited does not collect

We do not ourselves collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Account credentials (the App has no sign-up or login)
  • Precise location
  • Camera recordings or your photo library (used on-device only, as described in Section 3)
  • Payment card details (all payments are processed by the app store)
  • Any analytics or telemetry — the App includes no Varlextech Limited-operated analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting service

5. Third-party services

The App integrates the following third-party services. Each acts as an independent data controller and processes data under its own privacy policy.

5.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

QRForge displays banner and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and cap frequency, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only where you grant App Tracking Transparency permission)
  • IP address, from which coarse (city-level) location may be inferred
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, screen size, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions and taps)
  • Diagnostic and crash data related to the ad SDK

Personalized vs. non-personalized ads. Where required (e.g., in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), consent for personalized advertising is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow the first time you open the App, and can be changed at any time via your device or the consent options presented in the App. On iOS, the App requests App Tracking Transparency authorization before any tracking. You can also limit ad personalization globally via your device's advertising settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), or reset/delete your Advertising ID. Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes all advertising from the App.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's advertising technology partners: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

5.2 Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit (payments)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iOS) under the store's own terms and privacy policies. The store validates the transaction and the App records only a local flag that the purchase is active. Varlextech Limited never sees or stores your payment card information.

  • Google Play Terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/
  • Apple Media Services Terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/

  • Core features — scanning and decoding codes, generating QR codes, saving codes to your photo library, and maintaining your on-device history, favorites, and settings.
  • Advertising — displaying ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing ad fraud (AdMob).

For users in the EEA/UK, the legal bases are: performance of the contract (providing the App and the purchases you request), legitimate interests (fraud prevention and non-personalized advertising), and your consent (personalized advertising, where requested).
